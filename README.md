# shopping-behavior

## Analytical Questions

What are 6 analytical questions you can ask about this dataset?

1. Is there a relationship between demographics and seasonality in correspondence to the purchase amounts?

2. Does seasonality increase the purchase amounts?

3. Does gender determine the likelihood of what product the customer will buy?

4. Does the relationship between location and seasonality determine the likelihood of what product the customer will buy?

5. Does the seasons have an effect on the shipping type?

6. Does the customer age have an effect on what product they purchase?
